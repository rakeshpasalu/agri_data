Bhumi Intelligence — Executive Strategy Briefing

Autonomous Precision Agronomic Operating System · Technical Architecture

🎯 The Smallholder Decision Problem

Farmers in dryland agro-climatic zones (Central Dry Zone IV) operate under high climate volatility (450–700mm variable rainfall) and fragmented market signals. Traditional extension services provide generic district advice that fails to account for microclimate windows, soil texture, water availability, and statutory input safety.

💡 Evidence-Linked Biophysical Architecture

Bhumi integrates verified public datasets into a deterministic decision engine: Open-Meteo 14-day forecasts for operational spraying/sowing windows; FAO ECOCROP ecological envelopes; UAS Bengaluru & ICAR crop variety releases; CIB&RC statutory pesticide safety boundaries; and Karnataka Bhoomi cadastral verification gateways.

🛡️ 10-Year Phased Execution Roadmap

Phase 1 (Hosanagalapura Pilot): Calibrate the farmer decision loop with real field observations, farmer-declared water sources, and transparent missing-data handling.
Phase 2 (District Expansion): Integrate physical Soil Health Cards (SHC) and localized FPO/APMC market arrivals.
Phase 3 (Statewide Operating Network): Multi-season farmer-consented records powering transparent credit and crop insurance.
